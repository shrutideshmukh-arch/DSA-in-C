Frequently Asked Questions :

1.What are the different types of expressions?
Ans:Infix, Prefix, Postfix

2.Give examples of all types of expressions. 
Ans: Infix: a+b
Prefix: +ab 
Postfix: ab+

3.Which data structure is used for evaluation of expression?
Ans: Stack

4.Convert infix expression a+b+c+d to prefix expression. 
Ans:+++abcd

5.Convert infix expression a+b+c+d to postfix expression. 
Ans: ab+c+d+
